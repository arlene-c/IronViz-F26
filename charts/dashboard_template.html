          Glossary

          Token
          A small piece of text, about ¾ of a word. AI services measure and bill use in tokens.
          Cache
          Reusing the part of a prompt the model has seen recently. Cached tokens cost less.
          Premium model
          The larger, more expensive models: gpt-4o, o1-preview and gemini-1.5-pro.
          Rate limit (HTTP 429)
          A provider refusing a call because too many arrived at once. These calls are not billed.
          Budget rule
          The policy that decides, request by request, whether to spend money on it.
          Simulation
          Replaying the logged requests in arrival order and letting a budget rule approve or refuse each one, to see what would have happened.
          Simulated month
          A version of October rebuilt by resampling 12-hour blocks of requests. We ran 49 of them (Monte Carlo) to see how much results vary.
          Fully completed
          Approved and answered in full.
          Served at all
          Approved, whether the answer was complete or cut short.
          Cut short
          Approved, but the answer stopped early because it hit the output limit or the money set aside for it.
          Testing teams
          org-engineering-dev and prj-ai-sandbox-testing. Team groups are our reading of team names; the logs have no individual users.
          First come, first served
          Approve every request until the money runs out.
          Equal quotas
          Give each of the six teams an equal share of the budget.
          Training-week quotas
          Share the budget by each team's spending in the first week of October.
          Adaptive sharing
          Team shares, but a team can borrow money other teams aren't using.
          Spend pacing
          Approve a request only while total spending stays on a steady monthly pace.
          Cost threshold
          Refuse any single request expected to cost more than a limit set from the first week.
          Budget %
          Share of the modeled cost of serving every request from Oct 7 to Oct 30 ($ at 100%).
